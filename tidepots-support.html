Tidepots Support

Tidepots is a simple family timer app — run several timers at once for things like screen time, reading, cooking, and chores, and watch them fill up.

Do I need an account?

No. Tidepots works fully offline with no sign-in. Your timers live on your device. Signing in with Google or Apple is optional and only turns on cloud backup, so your timers come back if you reinstall the app or get a new phone.

How does cloud backup work?

Tap the cloud icon at the top of the app and sign in. Your timers back up automatically after changes. If you ever sign in on a fresh install with no timers, your backup is restored automatically.

How do notifications work?

Tidepots schedules a notification for when each timer finishes. If notifications don't arrive, open Settings > Tidepots > Notifications and make sure they're allowed.

How do I delete my account and data?

Tap the cloud icon, then “Delete account & data”. This permanently removes your account and cloud backup. Timers on your device remain until you delete the app.
