Chore Tracker Support

Chore Tracker keeps recurring household chores on schedule — set how often each chore repeats and get a gentle reminder the day before and the day it's due.

Do I need an account?

No. Chore Tracker works fully offline with no sign-in. Your chores live on your device. Signing in with Google or Apple is optional and only turns on cloud backup, so your chore list comes back if you reinstall the app or get a new phone.

How do reminders work?

Each chore is reminded the day before and on its due date, at the time you choose for that chore (tap the bell icon to set the default for new chores, or set a time per chore when adding or editing it). If reminders don't arrive, open Settings > Chore Tracker > Notifications and make sure they're allowed.

How does cloud backup work?

Tap the cloud icon at the top of the app and sign in. Your chore list backs up automatically after changes. If you ever sign in on a fresh install with no chores, your backup is restored automatically.

How do I delete my account and data?

Tap the cloud icon, then “Delete account & data”. This permanently removes your account and cloud backup. Chores on your device remain until you delete the app.
