Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Chore Tracker works fully without an account. Everything you enter stays on your device unless you choose to sign in for cloud backup.

If you sign in

Signing in with Google or Apple creates an account so we can back up your chore list. We store:

  • Your account identifier and email address (from Google or Apple)
  • A backup copy of your chore list (names, schedules, completion dates)

Backups are stored securely and are only accessible to you. We never sell your data, show ads, or use trackers.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and cloud backup at any time from the cloud backup panel in the app (tap the cloud icon, then “Delete account & data”). This permanently removes your account and backup. Chores stored on your device remain until you delete the app.
